1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & COMPLIANCE ASSURANCE
================================================================================

system-explorer is engineered under strict architectural and governance invariants:
100% Local-First, Zero-Egress, unprivileged user-mode execution (RunAsInvoker),
and fail-closed safety gate interception. The engine constructs evidence-backed
system cartographies, separates desired functions from concrete function carriers,
verifies cryptographic receipts, and identifies architecture drift.

All direct, optional, and development dependencies utilized across system-explorer
are distributed under strictly permissive open-source licenses (MIT, Apache-2.0,
BSD-3-Clause, PSFL-2.0). There are zero copyleft, GPL, or AGPL-style viral
dependencies, guaranteeing complete operational safety for enterprise
integration, autonomous agent fleets, and security-critical deployments.
Canonical copyright and ecosystem attributions are formally preserved in the
root NOTICE file.

3. ZERO-COPYLEFT ISOLATION GUARANTEE & RUNASINVOKER CERTIFICATION
================================================================================

1. Zero-Copyleft Isolation Guarantee:
   All core runtime dependencies operate strictly under permissive open-source
   licenses (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-3-Clause, PSFL-2.0). The codebase, build definitions,
   and runtime interfaces are completely free of GPL, AGPL, LGPL, SSPL, or
   reciprocal copyleft obligations.

2. Unprivileged Execution (RunAsInvoker):
   system-explorer enforces unprivileged user-mode execution under INV-NON-05.
   All repository scanning, AST parsing, SQLite mapping, and local web dashboard
   interfaces operate strictly within non-elevated user context without requiring
   administrative elevation, root privileges, or daemon background services.

3. Fail-Closed Security & Reversible Action Defense:
   Under INV-FAIL-03 and INV-RO-04, missing authority receipts, unknown claims, or
   signature mismatches immediately fail closed into a quarantine state.
   All mapping and proposal functions operate read-only and never mutate scanned
   codebases without explicit operator command.
